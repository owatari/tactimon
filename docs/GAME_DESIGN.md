# Game Design — Canonical Rules v0.1

Tactimon Online is a persistent multiplayer Pokémon fan MMORPG with a shared overworld and grid-based tactical battles.

## World and trainer

- Persistent multiplayer overworld beginning in Kanto/Pallet Town.
- Tall grass triggers encounter rolls; battles run in tactical instances while the world continues.
- Trainer is identity/cosmetics/social/navigation, not a combat-stat entity.
- Story follows towns, routes, gyms, badges, Elite Four and post-game, with redesigned teams and tactical arenas.

## Party and combat

- Party/deployment up to 6 Pokémon in normal encounters.
- Wild encounters can contain 3, 6, 8 or more enemies.
- Movement is 4-directional; orthogonal/Manhattan movement cost.
- Every Pokémon equips exactly 4 moves.
- Pokémon keeps level, Base Stats, IV, EV, Nature, Ability, STAB, effectiveness, crit, status, Held/Berry and four moves.
- Tactical layer adds AP, MP, range, shapes/AoE, line of sight, obstacles, displacement, zones and terrain.
- Speed primarily determines initiative, not movement distance.

## Dungeons

Authored, non-randomized exploration with puzzles and static blocking encounters. Solo/duo/trio players may split up and reunite in the boss room. Boss deployment always totals six Pokémon: solo 6; duo 3+3; trio 2+2+2.

## Raids

Direct boss battles with 6 players and 1 Pokémon per player. They assume trained Pokémon, movesets, IV/EV, Helds/Berries and encounter knowledge.

## Itemization

No item-level tiers. Specific TMs and Held Items belong to specific content so old activities remain useful. Chase drops are not guaranteed; XP, money, consumables/materials form a baseline reward layer.

Held Items permanently unlock their effect on the Pokémon they are bound to; that Pokémon can freely swap among its unlocked Held effects. Berries use the same slot, are consumable, and serve as accessible/weaker/conditional substitutes.
